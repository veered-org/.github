# Veered

Free browser games and small open-source tools. Nothing to install. Contact: [support@veered.org](mailto:support@veered.org)

These are tools I built with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) and use myself, shared in case they help others too. Most are MIT licensed; Crimson (consultation booking) and the KDE Connect fix (Scarlet) are GNU GPL v3. My law practice: [PatentLawNY.com](https://PatentLawNY.com). Jewish life and aliyah: [PatentlyJewish.com](https://PatentlyJewish.com).

> **Please test before relying on these tools.** They are shared as-is, with no warranty. They work on my own computers, but your system may differ. If something doesn't work, you can ask Claude (or another AI coding assistant) to look into it and let me know what you found, or simply email support@veered.org and I'll look into it.

## Games

- **[Merlot: Real-Time Multiplayer Tank Game](https://veered.org/merlot/)** (Merlot): a real-time strategy game for 1 to 12 players · [Play](https://merlot.veered.org)
- **[Maroon Isles Quest: Two-Player Co-op Text Adventure](https://veered.org/maroon/)** (Maroon): Maroon Isles Quest, a two-player co-op text adventure (parody) · [Play](https://quest.veered.org)
- **[Carmine (Swarm): Two-Player Real-Time Strategy Game](https://veered.org/carmine/)** (Carmine): Swarm Command, the two-player prototype for Merlot · [Play](https://swarm.veered.org)
- **[Trifecta: Card Game Rules (War, Egyptian Ratscrew, 1-2-3-4)](https://veered.org/trifecta/)** (Trifecta): a card game I made up, on its way to becoming an online game
- **[Veered Menu: DOS-Style Browser Game Launcher](https://veered.org/veered-menu/)** (Veered menu): the DOS-style menu at veered.org, and Oversimplified Breakout

## Tools

### Linux desktop, any distribution
- **[Linux Music Folder Player with Media Keys](https://veered.org/linux-music-player/)** (Cardinal): a shuffle music player for any folder, with media keys and resume ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/cardinal-music-player))
- **[Linux SMS and VOIP Phone App](https://veered.org/linux-sms-voip/)** (Cherry): SMS and VOIP phone calls from your Linux desktop, with any SIP provider ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/cherry-sms-calls))
- **[Linux Mouse Button Remapper for Wayland and X11](https://veered.org/linux-mouse-button-remap/)** (Rufous): map extra mouse buttons to keys, shortcuts and commands ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/rufous-mouse-remap))

### COSMIC desktop
- **[COSMIC Panel Tools: Word of the Day, Weather, Clocks and Music](https://veered.org/cosmic-panel-tools/)** (Redshift): COSMIC panel with language learning, time-zones, weather and music ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/redshift-cosmic-panel-bar))
- **[Screen Translator for Linux](https://veered.org/screen-translator-linux/)** (Rouge): drag over text anywhere on screen and read a translation ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/rouge-screen-translator))
- **[KDE Connect Fix for COSMIC (TLS Role Patch)](https://veered.org/kdeconnect-cosmic-fix/)** (Scarlet): a KDE Connect fix so your phone's link to COSMIC recovers by itself ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/scarlet-kdeconnect-fix))

### Jewish life
- **[Shabbos Zmanim Board for Chromecast](https://veered.org/shabbos-zmanim-board/)** (Flame): Shabbos Zmanim for Chromecast; also puts refrigerators in Shabbos mode and shows Israel emergency warnings ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/flame-shabbos-board))
- **[Self-Hosted Community Phone and Business Directory](https://veered.org/community-directory/)** (Terracotta): self-hosted community phone and business directory ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/terracotta-community-directory))

### Work
- **[USPTO Patent Center PDF Fixer and Scanned-PDF Shrinker](https://veered.org/uspto-pdf-tools/)** (Vermilion): patent PDF tools (USPTO page-size fixer and scanned-PDF shrinker) ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/vermilion-patent-pdf-tools))
- **[Braintree Drop-in Payment Page for PHP](https://veered.org/braintree-checkout/)** (Redwood): a Braintree checkout page where you enter your own keys ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/redwood-braintree-checkout))
- **[Easy!Appointments Add-ons: Blackouts, Reminders and Spam Filter](https://veered.org/easyappointments-addons/)** (Crimson): stripped-down form of Easy!Appointments with automatic blockout times, reminders, spam filter, and custom links to pay and join from email ([GitHub](https://github.com/veered-org/crimson-consultation-booking))
